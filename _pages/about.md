I am a final-year undergraduate student at the School of Accounting, Jiangxi University of Finance and Economics (JUFE, AACSB Accredited). My academic performance places me among the top of my class.

My research lies at the intersection of artificial intelligence and accounting, with a focus on developing multimodal foundation models for financial document understanding and intelligent audit systems. I have authored three first-author papers exploring the application of large language models and vision-language models to financial reporting, audit automation, investment decision support, and human–AI collaboration in accounting judgment. I have also been granted over 10 software copyright patents as the first author, covering AI-driven accounting assistants, AI tax robots, large-model-based financial analysis and forecasting systems, and automated audit systems—all aimed at bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI techniques and real-world financial workflows.

Beyond research, I have gained hands-on experience in the industry through three internships in Shanghai and Hangzhou. As a Financial AI Research Intern at ByteDance (June–September 2026), under the supervision of Gang Wang, I researched accounting task classification and human–AI collaboration, contributed to a preliminary coding exercise covering 20 accounting scenarios by analyzing classification agreement and documenting ambiguous cases and methodological limitations, and prepared a first-authored research manuscript together with reproducible analysis code and experimental documentation. As an Intelligent Finance Intern at Meituan (December 2025–March 2026), I supported financial reporting, budget consolidation, and tax filing workflows across 10+ subsidiaries. As a Financial Machine Learning Strategy Intern at Ant Group (December 2023–February 2024), I analyzed Alipay Huabei consumer credit behavior data to identify user patterns and credit risk characteristics, assisted in customer segmentation and credit stratification, and supported the evaluation of credit risk indicators and data-driven credit management strategies. I am also the recipient of the National Encouragement Scholarship and several enterprise-sponsored scholarships, in recognition of my academic excellence and research dedication.

I hold an <span class="highlight-red">IELTS score of 7.0</span> and the National Computer Rank Examination (Level 2). I am currently applying for graduate programs and actively seeking research internship opportunities in the fields of AI, multimodal machine learning, and financial technology. I am passionate about advancing the integration of artificial intelligence into accounting and finance, and I look forward to contributing to impactful research at the intersection of these disciplines.
